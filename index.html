How I work

Start with the problem. Finish with a working result.

I do not begin with a favourite technology. I first learn how the business works and what success should look like.

01

Understand

Talk to the people involved and map the real workflow, not only the first request.

02

Decide

Check risks and edge cases, compare options, and remove complexity that brings no value.

03

Build

Turn the agreed plan into software, integrations, data flows, and a usable interface.

04

Verify

Test real user journeys, fix failures, deploy, and keep improving the result.
